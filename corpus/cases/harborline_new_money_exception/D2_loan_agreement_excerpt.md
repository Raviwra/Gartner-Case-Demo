# D2. [SYNTHETIC] Draft Credit Agreement (Excerpt): Harborline Dental Partners, Inc.

Draft of Sept 30, 2026. Lender: Meridian Trust Bank. Borrower: Harborline Dental Partners, Inc. This excerpt reproduces selected sections of the draft agreement. Section numbers follow the draft. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this excerpt have the meanings given in the full draft agreement.

## §1.1 Selected definitions

"EBITDA" means, for any period, consolidated net income of the Borrower and its Subsidiaries for that period plus, to the extent deducted in determining net income, interest expense, income taxes, depreciation and amortization, and non-cash charges, each determined in accordance with GAAP; provided that EBITDA for any period that includes the Closing Date shall include the EBITDA of the Closing Date Acquisitions for the portion of that period before the Closing Date, as set out in the Quality of Earnings Report.

"Total Funded Debt" means, at any date, all indebtedness of the Borrower and its Subsidiaries for borrowed money, all capital lease obligations, and the Seller Note.

"Total Leverage Ratio" means, as of the last day of any fiscal quarter, the ratio of (a) Total Funded Debt on that day to (b) EBITDA for the four consecutive fiscal quarters then ended.

"Debt Service Coverage Ratio" means, for the four consecutive fiscal quarters then ended, the ratio of (a) EBITDA minus Unfinanced Capital Expenditures minus cash taxes paid to (b) scheduled payments of principal and interest on Total Funded Debt during that period.

"Closing Date Acquisitions" has the meaning given in Schedule 1.1. [Schedule 1.1 not included in this excerpt]

"Seller Note" means the subordinated promissory note to be issued by the Borrower to Dr. Nadia Fairweather as part of the purchase price for Silverpine Dental Studio, subordinated to the obligations under this Agreement on terms acceptable to the Lender.

"Selling Dentists" means Dr. Elliot Brandsma and Dr. Nadia Fairweather.

"Quality of Earnings Report" means the report on the Closing Date Acquisitions prepared by Ashgrove Advisory LLC and dated August 2026.

"Compliance Certificate" means a certificate signed by a financial officer of the Borrower that sets out the calculations of the Total Leverage Ratio and the Debt Service Coverage Ratio for the period then ended.

"Unfinanced Capital Expenditures" means, for any period, capital expenditures of the Borrower and its Subsidiaries paid in cash during that period, excluding any capital expenditures financed with the proceeds of indebtedness or capital leases.

"Lien" means any mortgage, pledge, security interest, charge or other encumbrance of any kind on any asset of the Borrower or any Subsidiary.

## §2.1 Facilities

(a) Term Loan. Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Lender agrees to make a term loan to the Borrower on the Closing Date in the principal amount of $17,000,000 (the "Term Loan"). The Term Loan matures on the date that is five years after the Closing Date and amortizes in equal quarterly installments on a twelve-year schedule, with the unpaid principal balance due on the maturity date.

(b) Revolving Commitment. Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Lender agrees to make revolving loans to the Borrower from time to time in an aggregate principal amount outstanding not to exceed $5,000,000 (the "Revolving Commitment"). The Revolving Commitment terminates, and all revolving loans are due, on the date that is five years after the Closing Date.

(c) Use of proceeds. The Borrower shall use the proceeds of the Term Loan only to refinance the existing indebtedness owed to Halvard Commercial Bank, to pay the cash purchase price of the Closing Date Acquisitions, and to pay fees and expenses in connection with this Agreement. The Borrower shall use the revolving loans to refinance the existing revolving loans owed to Halvard Commercial Bank and for working capital and general corporate purposes.

## §2.2 Security

The obligations of the Borrower under this Agreement are secured by (a) a first-priority security interest in all present and future assets of the Borrower and each Subsidiary, (b) a pledge of the equity interests in each Subsidiary, (c) a collateral assignment of the Borrower's management services agreements with its affiliated practices, to the extent permitted by applicable law, and (d) limited guaranties from Dr. Lena Marchetti and Owen Tasker, with recourse to the guarantors capped at $3,000,000 in the aggregate.

## §7.1 Maximum Total Leverage Ratio

"The Borrower shall not permit the Total Leverage Ratio, as of the last day of any fiscal quarter, to exceed 4.00 to 1.00; provided that for each fiscal quarter ending on or after December 31, 2027, the maximum shall be 3.75 to 1.00, and for each fiscal quarter ending on or after December 31, 2028, the maximum shall be 3.50 to 1.00."

## §7.2 Minimum Debt Service Coverage Ratio

"The Borrower shall not permit the Debt Service Coverage Ratio to be less than 1.20 to 1.00 as of the last day of any fiscal quarter."

## §7.3 Acquisitions

"The Borrower shall not, and shall not permit any Subsidiary to, acquire any dental practice or substantially all of the assets of any dental practice, other than the Closing Date Acquisitions and other acquisitions for which the cash purchase price does not exceed $2,000,000 individually and $5,000,000 in the aggregate in any fiscal year."

## §7.4 Financial Reporting

The Borrower shall deliver to the Lender:

(a) within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year, annual consolidated financial statements audited by an independent accounting firm acceptable to the Lender;

(b) within 45 days after the end of each of the first three fiscal quarters of each fiscal year, internally prepared quarterly consolidated financial statements;

(c) together with each delivery under clause (a) or (b), a Compliance Certificate signed by a financial officer of the Borrower, showing the calculations of the Total Leverage Ratio under §7.1 and the Debt Service Coverage Ratio under §7.2 for the period then ended; and

(d) within 45 days after the end of each fiscal quarter, a report of revenue by payer for that quarter.

## §7.5 Negative Pledge

(a) The Borrower shall not, and shall not permit any Subsidiary to, create, incur or permit to exist any Lien on any of its assets, except Permitted Liens.

(b) "Permitted Liens" means: (i) Liens in favor of the Lender; (ii) Liens securing capital lease obligations, limited to the dental equipment subject to those leases; (iii) purchase-money Liens securing indebtedness in an aggregate amount not exceeding $750,000 at any time outstanding; and (iv) Liens for taxes not yet due and payable.

## §7.6 Key Persons

"It shall be an Event of Default if Dr. Lena Marchetti ceases to serve as Chief Clinical Officer of the Borrower, or if the Selling Dentists, taken together, cease to practice at substantially the levels contemplated at the Closing Date, unless replacement dentists reasonably acceptable to the Lender are engaged within a reasonable time."
