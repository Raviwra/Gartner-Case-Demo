## Section 2: Borrower Overview and Relationship History

### Borrower Overview

Larkspur Precision Components, Inc. ("Larkspur") is privately held. [D5, ¶1] It was founded in 1994 in Rockford, Illinois, by Erik and Maria Halvorsen. [D5, ¶1]

Larkspur makes precision-machined components and welded assemblies for agricultural and construction equipment OEMs. [D5, ¶1] It has about 310 employees and one plant. [D5, ¶1]

Ownership is Erik Halvorsen 55%, Maria Halvorsen 25% and Anders Halvorsen (COO) 20%. [D5, ¶1]

Customer A accounted for 28% of FY2024 revenue and 31% of FY2025 revenue. [D1, §5] The top five customers accounted for 64% of FY2025 revenue. [D1, §5]

FY2023 to FY2025 financial statements were audited by Hartwell & Lo LLP, with unqualified opinions. [D1, header]

### Relationship History

Larkspur has been a deposit and treasury management customer of Meridian since 2019. [D5, ¶2] Average operating deposits were $4.1 million over the trailing 12 months to August 2026. [D5, ¶2] The relationship manager is P. Lindqvist. [D5, header]

A $12.0 million facility, consisting of a $7.0M term loan and a $5.0M revolver, was approved in June 2023. [D5, ¶2] The facility has been paid as agreed with no past dues. [D5, ¶2]

The Q4 2024 compliance certificate arrived 18 days late. [D5, ¶3] Credit Administration waived the reporting covenant (waiver W-25-0117). [D5, ¶3] All later certificates arrived on time. [D5, ¶3]

The FY2025 compliance certificate is dated April 28, 2026. [D1, §7] It reports compliance with both financial covenants under the existing 2023 credit agreement. [D1, §7]

### Current Request and Recent Contacts

The borrower wants to close by November 30, 2026, so construction can start before winter. [D5, ¶4] The borrower stated that a national bank has offered to deliver a term sheet within 10 business days. [D5, ¶4]
