## Section 2: Borrower Overview and Relationship History

### Company Profile
Larkspur Precision Components, Inc. is privately held. [D5, ¶1] It was founded in 1994 in Rockford, Illinois, by Erik and Maria Halvorsen. [D5, ¶1] The company makes precision-machined components and welded assemblies for agricultural and construction equipment OEMs. [D5, ¶1] It has about 310 employees and one plant. [D5, ¶1]
