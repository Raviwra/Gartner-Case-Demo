## Section 2: Borrower Overview and Relationship History

### Company Profile
Larkspur Precision Components, Inc. is privately held. [D5, ¶1] The company was founded in 1994 in Rockford, Illinois, by Erik and Maria Halvorsen. [D5, ¶1] It makes precision-machined components and welded assemblies for agricultural and construction equipment OEMs. [D5, ¶1] It has about 310 employees and one plant. [D5, ¶1]

### Ownership and Management
Ownership is Erik Halvorsen 55%, Maria Halvorsen 25% and Anders Halvorsen (COO) 20%. [D5, ¶1] The draft credit agreement defines "Founders" as Erik Halvorsen and Maria Halvorsen. [D2, §1.1]

On 2026-09-02, Erik Halvorsen (64) stated that he plans to hand the CEO role to Anders within three years. [D5, ¶5] The notes record that the family has discussed "informally" selling a minority stake to a private equity firm. [D5, ¶5] The notes record no timeline and no LOI. [D5, ¶5]

### Relationship History
Larkspur has been a deposit and treasury management customer of Meridian since 2019. [D5, ¶2] Average operating deposits were $4.1 million over the trailing 12 months to August 2026. [D5, ¶2]

A $12.0 million facility ($7.0M term loan, $5.0M revolver) was approved in June 2023. [D5, ¶2] The prior memo describes it as a new senior secured facility to refinance existing bank debt and fund working capital. [D3, Part B §1] The relationship notes record that the facility was paid as agreed with no past dues. [D5, ¶2]

The Q4 2024 compliance certificate arrived 18 days late. [D5, ¶3] Credit Administration waived the reporting covenant (waiver W-25-0117). [D5, ¶3] All later certificates arrived on time. [D5, ¶3]

### Current Request Context
On the 2026-08-19 call, Erik and Anders Halvorsen asked to increase the facility to $18.0 million. [D5, ¶4] The increase would fund a 60,000 sq ft expansion of the Rockford plant ($6.0 million), tied to a new multi-year Customer A program. [D5, ¶4] They want to close by November 30, 2026, so construction can start before winter. [D5, ¶4] They said a national bank has offered to deliver a term sheet within 10 business days. [D5, ¶4]
