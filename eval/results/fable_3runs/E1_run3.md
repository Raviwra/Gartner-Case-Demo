## Section 2: Borrower Overview and Relationship History

**Borrower profile**

Larkspur Precision Components, Inc. is a privately held company founded in 1994 in Rockford, Illinois, by Erik and Maria Halvorsen [D5, ¶1]. The company makes precision-machined components and welded assemblies for agricultural and construction equipment OEMs [D5, ¶1]. It operates from a single plant with about 310 employees [D5, ¶1]. The plant is located in Rockford, Illinois [D2, §2.2].

**Ownership and management**

Ownership is Erik Halvorsen 55%, Maria Halvorsen 25%, and Anders Halvorsen (COO) 20% [D5, ¶1]. Erik Halvorsen (64) plans to hand the CEO role to Anders Halvorsen within three years [D5, ¶5]. The family has discussed "informally" selling a minority stake to a private equity firm; there is no timeline and no LOI [D5, ¶5]. The draft credit agreement defines "Founders" as Erik Halvorsen and Maria Halvorsen [D2, §1.1].

**Relationship history**

Larkspur has been a deposit and treasury management customer of Meridian since 2019 [D5, ¶2]. Average operating deposits were $4.1 million over the trailing 12 months to August 2026 [D5, ¶2]. A $12.0 million facility ($7.0M term loan, $5.0M revolver) was approved in June 2023 [D5, ¶2]. The 2023 facility was a new senior secured facility to refinance existing bank debt and fund working capital [D3, Part B §1]. The loan has paid as agreed with no past dues [D5, ¶2].

On 2025-03-14, the Q4 2024 compliance certificate arrived 18 days late, and Credit Administration waived the reporting covenant (waiver W-25-0117) [D5, ¶3]. All later certificates arrived on time [D5, ¶3]. Per the FY2025 compliance certificate dated April 28, 2026, the borrower was in compliance with both financial covenants under the existing 2023 credit agreement [D1, §7].

**Current request**

On a 2026-08-19 call, Erik and Anders Halvorsen asked to increase the facility to $18.0 million to fund a 60,000 sq ft expansion of the Rockford plant ($6.0 million), tied to a new multi-year Customer A program [D5, ¶4]. They want to close by November 30, 2026, so construction can start before winter [D5, ¶4]. They said a national bank has offered to deliver a term sheet within 10 business days [D5, ¶4].
