## Section 2: Borrower Overview and Relationship History

**Borrower Overview**

Larkspur Precision Components, Inc. is a privately held company founded in 1994 in Rockford, Illinois [D3, Part B §2] [D5, ¶1]. The company was founded by Erik and Maria Halvorsen [D5, ¶1], who are defined as the "Founders" in the draft credit agreement [D2, §1.1]. Larkspur makes precision-machined components and welded assemblies for agricultural and construction equipment OEMs from a single plant [D3, Part B §2] [D5, ¶1]. The company has about 310 employees [D5, ¶1].

Ownership is Erik Halvorsen 55%, Maria Halvorsen 25%, and Anders Halvorsen (COO) 20% [D5, ¶1]. On a call dated 2026-09-02, Erik Halvorsen (64) stated that he plans to hand the CEO role to Anders within three years [D5, ¶5]. The family has discussed "informally" selling a minority stake to a private equity firm; there is no timeline and no LOI [D5, ¶5].

Customer A accounted for 28% of FY2024 revenue and 31% of FY2025 revenue [D1, §5]. The top five customers accounted for 64% of FY2025 revenue [D1, §5].

**Relationship History**

Larkspur has been a deposit and treasury management customer of Meridian since 2019 [D3, Part B §2] [D5, ¶2]. Average operating deposits were $4.1 million over the trailing 12 months to August 2026 [D5, ¶2].

A $12.0 million senior secured facility, consisting of a $7.0 million term loan and a $5.0 million revolving line of credit, was approved in June 2023 to refinance existing bank debt and fund working capital [D3, Part B §1] [D5, ¶2]. The relationship manager's notes record the facility as paid as agreed with no past dues [D5, ¶2]. At FY2025, the Meridian term loan balance was 5,600 and the Meridian revolver balance was 3,200 [D1, Table 4: Meridian term loan, FY2025; Meridian revolver, FY2025].

On 2025-03-14, the Q4 2024 compliance certificate arrived 18 days late, and Credit Administration waived the reporting covenant under waiver W-25-0117 [D5, ¶3]. All later certificates arrived on time [D5, ¶3]. Per the FY2025 compliance certificate dated April 28, 2026, the borrower was in compliance with both financial covenants under the existing 2023 credit agreement [D1, §7].

**Current Request**

On a call dated 2026-08-19, Erik and Anders Halvorsen asked to increase the facility to $18.0 million to fund a 60,000 sq ft expansion of the Rockford plant ($6.0 million), tied to a new multi-year Customer A program [D5, ¶4]. They want to close by November 30, 2026, so construction can start before winter [D5, ¶4]. They said a national bank has offered to deliver a term sheet within 10 business days [D5, ¶4].
