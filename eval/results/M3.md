### Items for legal and underwriter review

1. **§7.6 Change of Control, ambiguous terms (Low confidence).** "Meaningful portion" and "actively involved in the management" are quoted without interpretation; referred to legal and the underwriter [D2, §7.6]. Related cited facts: Ownership is Erik Halvorsen 55%, Maria Halvorsen 25%, Anders Halvorsen (COO) 20% [D5, ¶1]. Erik Halvorsen (64) plans to hand the CEO role to Anders within three years, and the family has discussed "informally" selling a minority stake to a private equity firm, with no timeline and no LOI [D5, ¶5]. "Founders" means Erik Halvorsen and Maria Halvorsen [D2, §1.1].

2. **Schedule 1.1 not provided.** "Expansion Project" has the meaning given in Schedule 1.1, which is not included in the excerpt [D2, §1.1]. This affects §7.3 and §2.1(c). Not in provided documents: Schedule 1.1 (full draft credit agreement).

3. **"Equipment Notes" undefined in excerpt.** The term appears in the definitions of Total Funded Debt and Unfinanced Capital Expenditures and in Permitted Liens [D2, §1.1], [D2, §7.5(b)]. D1 shows "Equipment notes (Prairie Equipment Finance)" of 11,000 in FY2025 [D1, §4] but the contractual definition is not in the provided documents.

4. **§7.3 testing mechanics.** The capex covenant is expressed "in any fiscal year" [D2, §7.3], but the Compliance Certificate requirement references only §7.1 and §7.2 [D2, §7.4(c)]. Legal to confirm how and when §7.3 compliance is certified.

5. **§7.4(a) lender discretion.** Auditor must be "acceptable to the Lender" [D2, §7.4(a)]. FY2023-FY2025 statements were audited by Hartwell & Lo LLP with unqualified opinions [D1, header]. Underwriter to confirm acceptability.

6. **Change in covenant levels versus existing agreement (facts only, no evaluation).** Existing 2023 agreement: maximum total leverage 3.50x, minimum DSCR 1.20x [D1, §7], [D3, Part B §4]. Draft agreement: maximum Total Leverage Ratio 3.25x, stepping to 3.00x for quarters ending on or after December 31, 2027; minimum DSCR 1.25x [D2, §7.1], [D2, §7.2]. Pro forma figures on an FY2025 basis: total leverage 2.52x, DSCR 1.38x [D1, Table 3: Total leverage, Pro forma], [D1, Table 3: DSCR, Pro forma]. Policy minimums: pro forma total leverage not exceeding 3.50x, pro forma DSCR at least 1.25x [D4, §4.2.1], [D4, §4.2.2].

7. **Definitional alignment between spread and agreement.** The D1 spread defines DSCR using "unfunded capex" [D1, §2, Definitions as spread]; the draft agreement uses "Unfinanced Capital Expenditures," which excludes capex financed with indebtedness (including the Equipment Notes) or Term Loan proceeds [D2, §1.1]. Underwriter to confirm whether the spread calculation matches the contractual definition. Not calculated here.

8. **Prior reporting covenant history.** The Q4 2024 compliance certificate arrived 18 days late and Credit Administration waived the reporting covenant (waiver W-25-0117); all later certificates arrived on time [D5, ¶3]. Relevant to covenants #5 and #6.

9. **Terms not reproduced in excerpt.** "Closing Date," "Compliance Certificate," "Permitted Liens" form, and any other covenants, events of default or cure periods outside §§2.1, 2.2, 7.1-7.6 are not in the provided documents (full draft credit agreement would contain them) [D2, header note].
