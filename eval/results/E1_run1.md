## Section 2: Borrower Overview and Relationship History

### Company Profile
Larkspur Precision Components, Inc. is privately held. [D5, ¶1] It was founded in 1994 in Rockford, Illinois, by Erik and Maria Halvorsen. [D5, ¶1] It makes precision-machined components and welded assemblies for agricultural and construction equipment OEMs. [D5, ¶1] It has about 310 employees and one plant. [D5, ¶1]

The FY2023-FY2025 financial statements were audited by Hartwell & Lo LLP, with unqualified opinions. [D1, header] The fiscal year ends December 31. [D1, header]

### Ownership and Management
Ownership: Erik Halvorsen 55%, Maria Halvorsen 25%, Anders Halvorsen (COO) 20%. [D5, ¶1] The draft agreement defines "Founders" as Erik Halvorsen and Maria Halvorsen. [D2, §1.1]

On a September 2, 2026 call, Erik Halvorsen (64) said he plans to hand the CEO role to Anders within three years. [D5, ¶2026-09-02 call] The notes record that the family has discussed "informally" selling a minority stake to a private equity firm, with no timeline and no LOI. [D5, ¶5]

### Customers
Customer A accounted for 28% of FY2024 revenue and 31% of FY2025 revenue. [D1, §5] The top five customers accounted for 64% of FY2025 revenue. [D1, §5]

### Relationship History with Meridian
Larkspur has been a deposit and treasury management customer since 2019. [D5, ¶2] Average operating deposits were $4.1 million over the trailing 12 months to August 2026. [D5, ¶2]

A $12.0 million facility ($7.0M term loan, $5.0M revolver) was approved in June 2023. [D5, ¶2] The prior memo describes it as a senior secured facility to refinance existing bank debt and fund working capital. [D3, Part B §1] The relationship is recorded as paid as agreed with no past dues. [D5, ¶2]

At FY2025, the Meridian term loan was 5,600 and the Meridian revolver was 3,200. [D1, §4]

Under the existing 2023 credit agreement, the FY2025 compliance certificate dated April 28, 2026 reports compliance with both financial covenants. [D1, §7]

The Q4 2024 compliance certificate arrived 18 days late. [D5, ¶3] Credit Administration waived the reporting covenant (waiver W-25-0117). [D5, ¶3] All later certificates arrived on time. [D5, ¶3]
