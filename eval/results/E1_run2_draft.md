## Section 2: Borrower Overview and Relationship History

### Company Profile

Larkspur Precision Components, Inc. is privately held. [D5, ¶1] The company was founded in 1994 in Rockford, Illinois, by Erik and Maria Halvorsen. [D5, ¶1] It makes precision-machined components and welded assemblies for agricultural and construction equipment OEMs. [D5, ¶1] The company has about 310 employees and one plant. [D5, ¶1]

Fiscal years end December 31. [D1, header] FY2023 through FY2025 were audited by Hartwell & Lo LLP, which issued unqualified opinions. [D1, header]

Ownership is Erik Halvorsen 55%, Maria Halvorsen 25%, and Anders Halvorsen (COO) 20%. [D5, ¶1]

On the 2026-09-02 call, Erik Halvorsen (64) stated that he plans to hand the CEO role to Anders within three years. [D5, ¶5] The family has discussed "informally" selling a minority stake to a private equity firm. [D5, ¶5] There is no timeline and no LOI. [D5, ¶5]

The draft agreement defines "Founders" as Erik Halvorsen and Maria Halvorsen. [D2, §1.1]
