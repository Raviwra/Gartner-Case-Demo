## §5 Customer and payer concentration

Payer A, a commercial dental benefits carrier, accounted for 36% of FY2024 revenue and 38% of FY2025 revenue. The top three payers accounted for 61% of FY2025 revenue. Medicaid dental programs accounted for 12% and patient self-pay for 17% of FY2025 revenue. No single patient or employer group accounted for more than 1% of revenue.

## §6 Management commentary

From the borrower's acquisition summary to the bank, as summarized by the analyst: "Revenue grew 8.8% in FY2025 on two de novo offices opened in 2024 and higher hygiene visit volume. With Brightmere and Silverpine, pro forma EBITDA is about $6.3 million. Management expects to move both practices onto the Harborline billing platform within six months of closing."

## §7 Existing covenant compliance

Per the FY2025 compliance certificate dated April 22, 2026, under the existing credit agreement with Halvard Commercial Bank: Total leverage was 2.88x against a maximum of 4.00x. DSCR was 1.52x against a minimum of 1.20x. The borrower was in compliance with both financial covenants.
