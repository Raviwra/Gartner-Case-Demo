## §5 Customer concentration

Customer A, a regional restaurant group, accounted for 21% of FY2024 revenue and 23% of FY2025 revenue. The top five customers accounted for 47% of FY2025 revenue.

## §6 Management commentary

From the borrower's FY2025 letter to the bank, as summarized by the analyst: "Revenue grew 4.8% in FY2025 on new independent restaurant accounts. Fuel and warehouse labor costs rose faster than case prices. EBITDA was about $4.5 million for the year. Management expects a route optimization project completed in May 2026 to reduce delivery cost per case."

## §7 Existing covenant compliance

Under the credit agreement dated December 15, 2022, the maximum total leverage ratio is 3.75x and the minimum DSCR is 1.20x, each tested quarterly.

Per the Q3 2025 compliance certificate dated November 14, 2025: Total leverage was 3.41x against a maximum of 3.75x. DSCR was 1.22x against a minimum of 1.20x.

Per the FY2025 compliance certificate dated April 24, 2026: Total leverage was 3.20x against a maximum of 3.75x. DSCR was 1.27x against a minimum of 1.20x.

Per the Q2 2026 compliance certificate dated August 12, 2026: Total leverage was 3.38x against a maximum of 3.75x. DSCR was 1.24x against a minimum of 1.20x.

Each certificate from Q1 2025 to Q2 2026 reported compliance with both financial covenants. The certificates do not report Excess Availability under the minimum availability covenant.
