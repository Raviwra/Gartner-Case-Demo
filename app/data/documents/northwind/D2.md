# D2. [SYNTHETIC] Credit Agreement (Excerpt): Northwind Foodservice Distribution, LLC

Credit Agreement dated December 15, 2022, conformed copy as in effect Sept 2026. Lender: Meridian Trust Bank. Borrower: Northwind Foodservice Distribution, LLC. This excerpt reproduces selected sections of the agreement. Section numbers follow the agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this excerpt have the meanings given in the full agreement.

## §1.1 Selected definitions

"EBITDA" means, for any period, consolidated net income of the Borrower for that period plus, to the extent deducted in determining net income, interest expense, income taxes, depreciation and amortization, and non-cash charges, each determined in accordance with GAAP.

"Total Funded Debt" means, at any date, all indebtedness of the Borrower for borrowed money, including the Revolving Loans, and all Capital Lease Obligations.

"Total Leverage Ratio" means, as of the last day of any fiscal quarter, the ratio of (a) Total Funded Debt on that day to (b) EBITDA for the four consecutive fiscal quarters then ended.

"Debt Service Coverage Ratio" means, for the four consecutive fiscal quarters then ended, the ratio of (a) EBITDA minus Unfinanced Capital Expenditures minus cash taxes paid (including Permitted Tax Distributions) to (b) scheduled payments of principal and interest on Total Funded Debt during that period, including interest on the Revolving Loans.

"Permitted Tax Distributions" means distributions to the members of the Borrower in an amount not exceeding their income tax liability attributable to the taxable income of the Borrower.

"Borrowing Base" means, at any date, the sum of (a) 85% of Eligible Accounts plus (b) the lesser of (i) 60% of Eligible Inventory valued at cost and (ii) $4,000,000, minus such reserves as the Lender may establish in its reasonable credit judgment.

"Eligible Accounts" means accounts receivable of the Borrower that are not more than 60 days past the invoice date and that are owed by account debtors located in the United States.

"Eligible Inventory" has the meaning given in Schedule 1.1. [Schedule 1.1 not included in this excerpt]

"Excess Availability" means, at any date, the lesser of the Revolving Commitment and the Borrowing Base, minus the aggregate principal amount of Revolving Loans then outstanding.

"Borrowing Base Certificate" means a certificate signed by a financial officer of the Borrower that sets out the calculation of the Borrowing Base as of the end of a calendar month.

"Compliance Certificate" means a certificate signed by a financial officer of the Borrower that sets out the calculations of the Total Leverage Ratio and the Debt Service Coverage Ratio for the period then ended.

"Unfinanced Capital Expenditures" means, for any period, capital expenditures of the Borrower paid in cash during that period, excluding any capital expenditures financed with Capital Lease Obligations or other indebtedness.

"Lien" means any mortgage, pledge, security interest, charge or other encumbrance of any kind on any asset of the Borrower.

## §2.1 Facilities

(a) Term Loan. The Lender made a term loan to the Borrower on the Closing Date in the principal amount of $4,000,000 (the "Term Loan"). The Term Loan is repayable in equal quarterly installments of $200,000, beginning March 31, 2023, with the unpaid principal balance due on December 15, 2027.

(b) Revolving Commitment. Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Lender agrees to make revolving loans to the Borrower from time to time (the "Revolving Loans") in an aggregate principal amount outstanding not to exceed the lesser of $10,000,000 (the "Revolving Commitment") and the Borrowing Base. The Revolving Commitment terminates, and all Revolving Loans are due, on December 15, 2027.

(c) Use of proceeds. The Borrower used the proceeds of the Term Loan to refinance indebtedness owed to its prior lender. The Borrower shall use the Revolving Loans for working capital and general corporate purposes.

## §2.2 Security

The obligations of the Borrower under this Agreement are secured by (a) a first-priority security interest in all present and future assets of the Borrower, including accounts, inventory and deposit accounts, (b) a landlord waiver from Ostrander Properties, LLC for the Borrower's leased distribution center in Des Moines, Iowa, and (c) a limited guaranty from Dale Ostrander, with recourse to the guarantor capped at $2,000,000 in the aggregate.

## §7.1 Maximum Total Leverage Ratio

"The Borrower shall not permit the Total Leverage Ratio, as of the last day of any fiscal quarter, to exceed 3.75 to 1.00."

## §7.2 Minimum Debt Service Coverage Ratio

"The Borrower shall not permit the Debt Service Coverage Ratio to be less than 1.20 to 1.00 as of the last day of any fiscal quarter."

## §7.3 Minimum Excess Availability

"The Borrower shall not permit Excess Availability to be less than $1,000,000 at any time."

## §7.4 Financial Reporting

The Borrower shall deliver to the Lender:

(a) within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year, annual financial statements audited by an independent accounting firm acceptable to the Lender;

(b) within 45 days after the end of each of the first three fiscal quarters of each fiscal year, internally prepared quarterly financial statements;

(c) together with each delivery under clause (a) or (b), a Compliance Certificate signed by a financial officer of the Borrower, showing the calculations of the Total Leverage Ratio under §7.1 and the Debt Service Coverage Ratio under §7.2 for the period then ended; and

(d) within 20 days after the end of each calendar month, a Borrowing Base Certificate as of the end of that month.

## §7.5 Negative Pledge

(a) The Borrower shall not create, incur or permit to exist any Lien on any of its assets, except Permitted Liens.

(b) "Permitted Liens" means: (i) Liens in favor of the Lender; (ii) Liens securing Capital Lease Obligations, limited to the vehicles and equipment subject to those leases; (iii) purchase-money Liens securing indebtedness in an aggregate amount not exceeding $500,000 at any time outstanding; and (iv) Liens for taxes not yet due and payable.

## §7.6 Change of Control

"It shall be an Event of Default if the members of the Borrower on the Closing Date cease to control the Borrower, or if Dale Ostrander ceases to have primary responsibility for the operations of the Borrower and a successor reasonably satisfactory to the Lender is not appointed within a reasonable period."
