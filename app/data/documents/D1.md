## §5 Customer concentration

Customer A accounted for 28% of FY2024 revenue and 31% of FY2025 revenue. The top five customers accounted for 64% of FY2025 revenue.

## §6 Management commentary

From the borrower's FY2025 letter to the bank, as summarized by the analyst: "Revenue grew 8.5% in FY2025 on new agricultural OEM programs. EBITDA margin improved on cell automation installed in 2024. Net income rose to $4.2 million. Management expects the plant expansion to add capacity for the Customer A program beginning in 2027."

## §7 Existing covenant compliance

Per the FY2025 compliance certificate dated April 28, 2026, under the existing 2023 credit agreement: Total leverage was 1.90x against a maximum of 3.50x. DSCR was 1.61x against a minimum of 1.20x. The borrower was in compliance with both financial covenants.
