# D3. Meridian Commercial Credit Memo Template v1 and Prior Memo Exemplar

This document has two parts. Part A is the credit memo template. Part B is the prior Larkspur memo (June 2023), re-cut into the Part A template so that it serves as a house-style exemplar.

## Part A: Meridian Commercial Credit Memo Template v1

Status: assumed template for this exercise, to be agreed with the senior underwriting team before use.

Header fields: Borrower | Request type | Total commitment | Underwriter | Senior reviewer | Date

### Author markers

- [M] Machine-assembled. A drafting tool may assemble the section from source documents. Every fact carries a citation.
- [U] Underwriter only. Authored by the underwriter. May not be drafted or pre-populated by any tool.
- [H] Hybrid. Subsections are marked individually as [M] or [U].

### Section list

| # | Section | Author |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Transaction Summary | [M] machine-assembled, cited |
| 2 | Borrower Overview and Relationship History | [M] machine-assembled, cited |
| 3 | Financial Analysis | [H] 3.1-3.4 machine-assembled and cited; 3.5 Underwriter Commentary by the underwriter |
| 4 | Covenant Summary and Compliance | [M] machine-assembled, cited |
| 5 | Collateral and Guaranty | [M] machine-assembled, cited |
| 6 | Policy Compliance and Exceptions | [M] lists the rules and the figures; a human approves any exception |
| 7 | Key Risks and Mitigants | [U] underwriter-authored |
| 8 | Underwriter Analysis and Recommendation | [U] underwriter-authored |
| 9 | Risk Rating and Rationale (underwriter only; required) | [U] May not be drafted or pre-populated by any tool. Left blank in this template. |
| 10 | Conditions and Approvals | [U] underwriter-authored |

### Section 3 subsections

- 3.1 Revenue and Earnings [M]
- 3.2 Leverage and Debt Service [M]
- 3.3 Liquidity [M]
- 3.4 Pro Forma for Proposed Facility [M]
- 3.5 Underwriter Commentary [U]

### Section 9 content

Section 9, Risk Rating and Rationale, is left blank in this template. It is required in every memo and is completed by the underwriter only.

### Style rules

- Third person.
- $ in thousands, matching the source.
- Fiscal-year labels in the form "FY2025".
- [M] sections carry no evaluative adjectives (strong, weak, solid, healthy). Evaluation belongs in [U] sections.
